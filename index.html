Red lentil soup

A cheap, filling soup for the middle of the week. Red lentils cook down on their own, so there's no soaking and not much chopping.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 200 g red lentils, rinsed
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 L vegetable stock
  • ¾ tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • ⅓ cup plain yoghurt, to serve

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a large pot over a medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook, stirring now and then, for 8 minutes, until soft.
  2. Stir in the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute, until it smells toasty.
  3. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, until the lentils have fallen apart.
  4. Blend about half of the soup, or all of it if you like it smooth.
  5. Stir in the salt and lemon juice. Taste, and add more salt if it needs it.
  6. Serve with a spoonful of yoghurt on top.

Notes

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. It thickens as it sits, so add a splash of water when you reheat it.
